Privacy Policy

Shorts Factory · Last updated: 9 October 2026

Shorts Factory is a personal desktop application. Its only user is its developer, who uses it with the developer's own YouTube channels. This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • Basic information about the authorized YouTube channel (channel name and ID, list of uploaded videos).
  • YouTube Analytics metrics of that channel's own videos (views, engaged views, average view duration and percentage, audience retention, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained).
  • Permission to upload and schedule videos to that channel.

How the data is used

Only to show the channel owner how each video performs, to decide the format of future videos, and to upload the owner's own videos. Aggregated statistics may be processed with AI assistants (Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude) solely to help the owner with that decision. The data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored locally on the owner's computer: the access tokens and the statistics. Nothing is stored on any server. Statistics are refreshed regularly and can be deleted at any time.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the application's local folder removes all stored tokens and data.
